Terminal Towersengineering works in a shared workspace

Terminal Towers, in the Axis Towers complex, is a modern shared workspace combining open-plan work areas, private offices, and meeting and conference rooms.

Biomi Holding carried out the relevant engineering works for the heating, cooling and ventilation systems.

Project specifics

The work covered both exposed ductwork in the open, loft-style areas and integrating the systems into zones with closed ceilings. Spaces with different architectural treatments and different functions each demanded their own approach.

The solution

Open work areas, private offices and conference rooms create different loads and air-distribution requirements. Biomi Holding's execution was fitted to exactly those functional, technical and architectural specifics.
