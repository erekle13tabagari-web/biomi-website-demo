Deploying a VRF system in a multi-functional center

How we delivered climate control for 12,000 m² of retail and office space with a single integrated VRF system — from design to commissioning in five months.

A multi-functional center is one of the most demanding types of facility for climate control: retail space, offices, restaurants and conference halls each have radically different temperature regimes and load schedules. That is exactly why we chose the VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow) system for this project — the only technology that gives every zone independent control.

12,000 m²total area
4outdoor units
86indoor units
35%energy-cost savings

The challenge

The building's architecture left no room for the large cross-sections of traditional air ducts, and placing outdoor units on the facade was not permitted. On top of that came a requirement: the system had to run continuously, without disrupting the shopping center's operating hours.

  • We placed the outdoor units on the roof, with noise insulation
  • We ran the refrigerant lines through the existing utility shafts
  • Installation was carried out at night — the center never closed for a single day

The result

The system was commissioned two weeks ahead of schedule. Thanks to the Heat Recovery configuration, in winter the north-facing offices are warmed by the heat that the south-facing retail space gives off — which fed directly into lower operating costs.

"In its very first season the facility consumed 35% less electricity than the design calculations had projected for a traditional chiller scheme." — Biomi engineering team

The facility is served by Biomi's 24/7 technical support: every system parameter is monitored remotely from the BMS platform.
