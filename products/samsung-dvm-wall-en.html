A straightforward wall unit for a VRF loop — without the WindFree function, but with the same durability: self-cleaning, an anti-bacterial filter and a corrosion-protected heat exchanger.

Faster, wider, farther

A wide outlet sends the stream further, so the room cools quickly and evenly.

Automatic self-cleaning

After shutdown the unit dries itself, holding off damp and odour with no manual step.

Corrosion protection

The heat exchanger coating slows wear in damp, salty air — which matters on coastal sites.
