One outdoor unit - several rooms

From 2 to 5 indoor units on a single condenser. The façade stays clean, plant space is saved, and each room is controlled independently of the others.

Twin BLDC inverter compressor

Two rotors share the load - less vibration, smoother running and high efficiency at part load, which is where the system spends most of its time.

Wide operating temperature range

The unit runs across a broad range of outdoor temperatures - through summer heat and winter frost alike, holding heating mode.
