PUMY VRF Outdoor Unit

City Multi · R410A
Mitsubishi Electric's VRF system
Model

A compact City Multi VRF outdoor unit from 9.0 to 33.5 kW - for houses, apartments, shops and offices. Up to 25 indoor units connect to one outdoor unit: City Multi units directly, and M Series and Mr Slim units (MSZ-LN, MSZ-AP, SEZ-M, SLZ-M and others) through a branch box.

SP models have one fan, P models two. With the optional air protection guide, cooling runs down to -15 °C.

Compatible indoor units

City Multi: PLFY, PEFY, PKFY, PFFY, PCFY, PMFY - directly, with branch joints. M Series and Mr Slim: MSZ-LN, MSZ-EF, MSZ-AP, MFZ-KW, SLZ-M, PLA-M, PCA-M, SEZ-M, PEAD-M - through a branch box. Both kinds can share one system. Which units connect depends on the outdoor unit's capacity - we will put the combination together for you.

Accessories

Branch boxes: PAC-MK34BC, PAC-MK54BC (up to 3 boxes on P250/P300) · Air protection guide: PAC-SH95AG-E (up to P200), PAC-SK21AG-E (P250/P300) · Quiet mode, up to 10 dB(A) lower: PAC-SC36NA-E (P250/P300).

CE · RoHS · ISO 9001. Full certification available on request.
