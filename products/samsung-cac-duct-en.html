Samsung · CAC · Ducted

Ducted indoor unit (Slim Duct)

Model: AC071BNMDEH · series AC071–AC140

A concealed, ceiling-mounted ducted indoor unit that blends invisibly into the interior and distributes air through ducts to several spaces. Ideal for offices, retail and residential projects where a discreet, hidden solution is preferred.

Request a quote Samsung — all series
Features

What the ducted series offers

Invisible installation, efficiency and even comfort.

Concealed installation

The unit hides entirely in the ceiling — only discreet grilles remain visible.

Energy efficiency

Inverter technology with world-class efficiency — low running costs.

Even cooling

Automatic, uniform air distribution across the whole area — no temperature differences.

Simple to service

Convenient servicing in various locations — easy access to components.

Quiet operation

Low noise levels — invisible, unobtrusive comfort day and night.

Centralized control

Compatible with wired/wireless controllers and BMS integration.

World-class efficiency

The inverter compressor draws only the energy needed and holds a stable temperature — which feeds directly into lower running costs.

Simple set-up, even cooling

The system automatically balances the airflow and distributes it evenly, which simplifies commissioning and creates uniform comfort across every zone.

Convenient servicing

The design is built for a range of installation conditions, making preventive maintenance and service easier for technical staff.
