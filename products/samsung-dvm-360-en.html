DVM S 360° Cassette indoor unit

Features

What DVM S 360° offers

Circular design and smart air management — comfort in every corner.

360° airflow

Air is distributed evenly in every direction across the room — no cold or hot spots.

Bladeless design

No visible blades — air moves smoothly, without the discomfort of a direct draught.

Booster fan

An extra airflow mode for faster, more effective air delivery in larger spaces.

Circular LED display

An integrated illuminated indicator — easy visual control of mode and status.

Built-in drain pump

Reliable condensate removal — flexible installation across different ceiling layouts.

Turbo fan

An efficient turbo fan with low noise — quiet operation down to 30 dBA.

Fits any interior

A circular, minimalist form sits naturally in modern ceilings — office, boutique or hotel lobby. The design blends in rather than drawing attention, while quietly delivering comfort.

Air — in every direction

The 360° outlet spreads air evenly throughout the space, reducing temperature differences and creating a stable, comfortable environment in every part of the room.

Simple, intuitive control

Compatible with Samsung's wired and wireless controllers as well as centralized and BMS control — the whole system is managed from a single interface.
