About us

The engineering of how a space works

Biomi Holding JSC is an engineering holding that unites heating, cooling, ventilation and water-supply into one complete, integrated system.

Biomi is a balanced ecosystem: air, temperature and water are interconnected elements that together create a unified, efficient and stable environment. We build energy-efficient systems that work seamlessly — with maximum precision and efficiency.
